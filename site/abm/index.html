DEP / ABM Enrollment Submission

Submit serial numbers for enrollment in your customer's Apple Business Manager. GatorTec forwards the request to TD Synnex for processing.

Your customer must first add GatorTec as a Device Supplier in their Apple Business Manager. Our Reseller ID is 5559440.

View Apple's instructions for adding a Device Supplier →

Note: At this time we can only support enrollments for hardware purchased through TD Synnex. For orders from other distributors, please contact us directly.

Your customer's Apple Business Manager Organization ID — typically a 7-digit number found in their ABM account settings.
One serial per line — or comma-separated on a single line. Each device becomes a separate row in the enrollment request. Apple serials are 10–12 characters (letters + numbers only, no descriptions or notes).
We'll email you when the enrollment is processed by TD Synnex (usually within one business day).

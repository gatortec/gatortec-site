Financing

Own more Mac for less month-to-month.

Apple hardware costs have climbed — leasing is how many teams keep up without straining cash flow. Spread the cost of new Macs, iPads, and Apple accessories over 24, 36, or 48 months, deploy sooner, and keep your working capital where it belongs.

$1 Buyout

You own the equipment at the end of the term for exactly one dollar. Slightly higher monthly payments but the hardware is yours to keep. Best when you plan to hold the devices for their full useful life.

Fair Market Value (FMV)

Lower monthly payments during the term. At the end you can return the hardware, renew the lease, or purchase the equipment at its fair market value. Best when you plan to refresh your fleet on a schedule.

Both structures may offer tax advantages. Please consult your accountant or tax advisor about the treatment that fits your business.

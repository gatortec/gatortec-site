The magic of Active Noise Cancellation — now on standard AirPods. A redesigned acoustic architecture for richer sound. Live Translation, Siri AI, and Personalized Spatial Audio. Up to 22 hours of total listening time.

Key features

What makes it special.

  • Active Noise Cancellation

    Improved Active Noise Cancellation reduces outside noise — an industry first at this price. Perfect for commutes, focus time, or travel.

  • Adaptive Audio + Transparency

    Blend Active Noise Cancellation and Transparency for the best experience in any environment. Conversation Awareness lowers your media when you start talking.

  • Live Translation

    Communicate across languages using Live Translation, enabled by Apple Intelligence.

  • Siri AI

    Hands-free access to a more powerful, personalized AI assistant. Ask open-ended questions and get more done — right from your ears.

  • Personalized Spatial Audio

    Personalized Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking places sound all around you — like a theater in your ears.

  • IP57 rated

    Dust, sweat, and water resistant — designed to handle rain and heavy workouts.

  • Force sensor with volume swipe

    Easily adjust volume without taking out your iPhone, on the AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case model.

  • Long battery life

    Up to 5 hours of listening with ANC on a single charge. Up to 22 hours of total listening time with the charging case.

  • USB-C + wireless charging

    The Wireless Charging Case works over USB-C, plus Apple Watch and Qi chargers.

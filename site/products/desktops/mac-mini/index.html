Mac mini · M6 and M5 Pro chips

Mac mini. Little do-it-all.

Everything you do on Mac mini feels more responsive with the M6 chip and its next-generation CPU. Fly through AI workflows with up to 4.8x faster AI performance,1 up to 32GB of unified memory, and Wi-Fi 7. Five by five inches of pure power — with ports on the front and back.

At a glance

Small footprint, big features.

Key features

Fits, flexes, and flies in any setup.

  • M6 chip — next-generation performance

    A Neural Accelerator in each GPU core and faster unified memory deliver up to 4.8x faster AI performance than the previous generation.1 Fly through AI workflows, on device.

  • Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6

    The Apple-designed N1 wireless chip brings Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 to Mac mini — fast, stable wireless without a dongle in sight.

  • Connect it all

    Three Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, and 2.5Gb Ethernet on the back. Two USB-C ports and a headphone jack on the front. Supports up to three external displays.

  • Memory and storage that keep up

    Up to 32GB of unified memory, up to 170GB/s memory bandwidth, and up to 2x faster storage — so large media files and AI workloads move without waiting.

  • Step up to M5 Pro

    Choose the M5 Pro configuration for a 15-core CPU and 16-core GPU when your workflow calls for more headroom on demanding projects.

  • Looks small. Lives large.

    Just 5 by 5 inches. Fits perfectly under a monitor or slides in on any desk. Industry-leading power efficiency for always-on workloads.

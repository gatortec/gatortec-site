Mac Studio · M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips

Mac Studio. Brawn of a new age.

A pro desktop built to whisper through AI workloads. The M5 Max chip brings a next-generation CPU, a more powerful GPU with third-generation ray tracing, and a Neural Accelerator built into each GPU core. Configure with M5 Ultra for up to 512GB of unified memory and 1.2TB/s of bandwidth.1

Starting at $2,499

At a glance

Pro power, compact form.

Key features

Pro performance, pro everything.

  • M5 Max chip — pro AI, right on your desk

    Next-generation CPU, more powerful GPU with third-generation ray tracing, and a Neural Accelerator in every GPU core. Real-time media generation and complex creative workflows fly.

  • M5 Ultra — two Max chips fused

    Two M5 Max chips fused into one for extreme performance. Cluster with additional Mac Studios to unlock even bigger AI compute.

  • Phenomenal memory and storage

    Up to 128GB unified memory with M5 Max. Up to 512GB unified memory and 1.2TB/s bandwidth with M5 Ultra. Storage is up to 2x faster for dense file transfers.2

  • Thunderbolt 5 everywhere

    Four Thunderbolt 5 ports on the back (M5 Max) or six total on M5 Ultra. Up to 120Gb/s per port for clustered AI compute or PCIe expansion.

  • Connect all the things

    USB-A, HDMI 2.1, SDXC card slot, 10Gb Ethernet, headphone jack. Supports up to five external displays (M5 Max) or eight (M5 Ultra).

  • Fits right on your desk

    Compact 7.7-inch-square enclosure fits under most displays. The advanced thermal system keeps Mac Studio quiet under load — never interferes with your workflow.

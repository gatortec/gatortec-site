Apple Watch Series 12. A work of heart.

The most accurate heart rate sensing in a wearable. HRV readings every five minutes. A Readiness score to help you know when to push and when to rest. Ceramic Shield 2 on aluminum cases. And all-day battery — up to 24 hours.

Key features

What makes it special.

  • Most accurate heart rate sensor in a wearable

    Series 12 now measures HRV as often as every five minutes for insights into stress and recovery — with the most accurate heart rate sensing Apple has ever put in a watch.

  • Readiness score

    A single, clear score that helps you decide when to push and when to rest — combining sleep, HRV, and recent training load.

  • Vitals app + sleep score2

    Overnight metrics — heart rate, respiratory rate, wrist temperature, blood oxygen1, and sleep duration — surfaced in one place. Plus a nightly sleep score and sleep apnea notifications.

  • Hypertension notifications2

    Series 12 can analyze how your blood vessels respond to your heartbeats over 30 days and notify you of patterns consistent with chronic hypertension.

  • Siri AI + Audio Intelligence

    Natural, back-and-forth conversations with Siri right from your wrist. Audio Intelligence adapts what you hear to your environment.

  • Ceramic Shield 2 & new case materials

    Aluminum cases now have Ceramic Shield 2 — tougher than any smartwatch glass. Choose aluminum, titanium, or a new ceramic case with a Sapphire display.

  • All-day battery, fast charge

    Up to 24 hours of normal use, and 15 minutes on the charger gets you back to a full day.

  • Crash and Fall Detection

    Apple Watch can detect a severe car crash or a hard fall and connect you to emergency services.3

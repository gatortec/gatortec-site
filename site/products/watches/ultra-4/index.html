Key features

What makes it special.

  • Multi-day battery — up to 45 hours in Max Endurance

    Ultramarathon battery stamina. Perfect for long trail days, expeditions, or just skipping the nightly charge.

  • Most accurate heart rate sensor in a wearable

    Ultra 4 measures HRV as often as every five minutes — for deeper insights into stress, recovery, and training load.

  • Readiness score

    One clear score to help you decide when to push hard and when to rest — combining sleep, HRV, and recent training load.

  • Titanium case, 49mm

    Aerospace-grade titanium is light, strong, and corrosion-resistant. Choose Black or Natural Titanium.

  • Siri AI + Audio Intelligence

    Natural, back-and-forth conversations with Siri right from your wrist. Audio Intelligence adapts what you hear to your environment.

  • Precision dual-frequency GPS

    L1 + L5 dual-frequency GPS for the most accurate location data — even in cities or under tree cover.

  • Recreational diving to 40m

    Ultra 4 works as a dive computer with compatible apps. Water-resistant to 100m. EN 13319 compliant.2

  • Advanced health & safety

    Hypertension notifications, sleep apnea notifications, ECG, Blood Oxygen,3 and Crash and Fall Detection connect you to help fast — including satellite emergency messaging when you're off the grid.4

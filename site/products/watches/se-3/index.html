Apple Watch SE 3

Apple Watch SE 3. Essential features. Great value.

Advanced health tracking — now with temperature sensing, sleep score, and advanced cycle tracking. Siri AI on your wrist. Fast charging that goes from empty to a full day in a hurry. Crash and Fall Detection to keep help close.

Starting at $249

Key features

What makes it special.

  • Temperature sensing (new to SE)

    SE 3 adds wrist temperature sensing — powering deeper insights in the Vitals app and advanced Cycle Tracking with retrospective ovulation estimates.

  • Sleep score1

    A nightly Sleep Score helps you understand your sleep quality — and see how it changes over time.

  • Siri AI

    Ask Siri to help you find what you need, brainstorm ideas, or take action — right from your wrist.

  • Fast charging

    Charges up to twice as fast as the previous SE. 18 hours of all-day battery — sunup to sundown.

  • Crash and Fall Detection

    SE 3 can detect a hard fall or severe car crash and automatically help connect you with emergency services.2

  • Great fitness tracker

    Track workouts your way — running, strength training, walking, and more. Hit your daily 10K steps and close your rings.

  • Apple Watch For Your Kids

    Set up Apple Watch For Your Kids, even if they don't have their own iPhone. Great for staying in touch and building good habits.

  • Cellular option

    Stay connected without your iPhone — take calls, send texts, stream music. Or choose GPS and pair with iPhone or Wi-Fi.
